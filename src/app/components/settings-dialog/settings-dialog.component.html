Prerequisites

Use prerequisites
Connections gate their target — a node stays locked and dimmed until everything pointing into it is completed. Turn off to use connections as plain, non-gating links.

Connections

Insert on drop
Hold a dragged node or group over a connection for a moment, then drop, to insert it in between — A → B becomes A → dropped → B. Turn off to drop over connections without changing them.
